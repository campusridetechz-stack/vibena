How creator earnings will work

Separate balances help protect users and VIBENA.

Gifts from listeners: eligible gifts can generate creator earnings based on a published reward rate.

Self-gifting: may earn a separate, lower reward rate, with daily limits and anti-fraud checks. The rate is not final.

Streaming rewards: any two-hour host reward will be a separate, funded programme—not an automatic promise.

Purchased VIBENA Coins will never automatically convert into withdrawable cash. Refunded, fraudulent or reversed transactions will not qualify.
